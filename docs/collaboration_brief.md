# Collaboration brief

## Question for expert review

Can public transcriptomic evidence distinguish suppressible immune-effector activity from persistent
endothelial or parenchymal injury in kidney-allograft ABMR?

## Specific review requested from Prof. Minnie Sarwal or another transplant expert

1. Are DSA-positive/no-rejection biopsies biologically interpretable as an intermediate state, or too
   heterogeneous for that framing?
2. Which published molecular scores best separate NK/IFN activity from endothelial injury?
3. Are GSE36059 ABMR/TCMR labels adequate for specificity analysis without re-adjudication?
4. Which cell classes should be prespecified for pseudobulk localization?
5. What result would falsify the proposed two-component model?
6. Which claims would be clinically misleading despite statistical significance?

The requested contribution is critical review of assumptions and interpretation, not endorsement,
private data access or wet-lab validation.
