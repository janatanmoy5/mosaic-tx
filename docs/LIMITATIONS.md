# Limitations and non-claims

- Historical GEO labels are not re-adjudicated under a uniform contemporary Banff standard.
- Cross-platform probe mapping and normalization can create residual technical heterogeneity.
- GSE50084 public metadata do not establish a safe patient-level blood/biopsy pairing.
- GSE275824 has 22 patients; pathway effects can be detectable when individual-gene effects are not.
- Public single-cell donor counts are modest and susceptible to center, dissociation and treatment effects.
- Available spatial transplant data do not provide an adequately powered independent ABMR cohort.
- Transcriptomic association and treatment response do not establish causal target biology.
- This study cannot demonstrate diagnostic utility, treatment efficacy, patient benefit or safety.
- Docking, MD and drug-ranking outputs would not repair these limitations and are not primary analyses.
