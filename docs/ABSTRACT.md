# Abstract

## Reference-aware qualification of immune and endothelial transcript programs in antibody-mediated kidney allograft rejection

**Background:** ABMR remains a major cause of kidney-allograft failure, but DSA exposure, active rejection, TCMR, and treatment response are often conflated. We asked which graft transcript programs distinguish damaging rejection from DSA exposure alone, replicate independently, differ from TCMR, and respond to a randomized ABMR-directed intervention.

**Methods:** We reanalyzed three public cohorts without pooling platforms: GSE50084 for DSA-conditioned discovery (61 biopsies), GSE36059 for replication and TCMR specificity (381 biopsies), and GSE275824 for randomized longitudinal response (63 biopsies from 22 patients). Platform-specific probes were mapped to genes. Prespecified FCGR3A/Fc, NK, cytotoxicity, interferon-gamma/chemokine, endothelial-activation, and tissue-injury modules were means of within-cohort gene z-scores. Limma models estimated cross-sectional effects; patient-blocked difference-in-differences and random-intercept mixed models estimated longitudinal effects. Hallmark GSEA and descriptive platform-specific PCA were secondary analyses. BH FDR correction was applied within each contrast family.

**Results:** In GSE50084, DSA-positive rejection versus DSA-positive non-rejection was associated with cytotoxicity (standardized difference 0.325, 95% CI 0.103-0.546; FDR=0.020), endothelial activation (0.428, 0.122-0.734; FDR=0.020), FCGR3A/Fc signaling (0.264, 0.033-0.494; FDR=0.045), interferon-gamma/chemokines (0.557, 0.195-0.920; FDR=0.020), and NK identity (0.297, 0.093-0.501; FDR=0.020). DSA positivity without rejection did not significantly alter these modules. All five programs replicated in ABMR versus non-rejection in GSE36059. However, FCGR3A/Fc signaling, cytotoxicity, and interferon-gamma/chemokines were higher in TCMR than ABMR, failing the ABMR-specificity gate. In GSE275824, felzartamab produced relative week-24 reductions in cytotoxicity (difference-in-differences -0.915; FDR=0.025), interferon-gamma/chemokines (-1.122; FDR=0.009), and NK identity (-0.914; FDR=0.025), but not endothelial activation (-0.539; FDR=0.103). Deposited MMDx transcript-derived scores showed concordant reductions in ABMR activity (interaction beta=-0.798; FDR=0.005), ABMR interferon-gamma (beta=-0.866; FDR=0.013), and ABMR NK (beta=-0.522; FDR=0.019). No tested program remained significant at week 52.

**Conclusions:** Multicohort evidence supports a transiently treatment-responsive immune program accompanying damaging DSA-associated rejection, but several components are shared with TCMR and are not ABMR-specific biomarkers. The weaker endothelial response motivates a two-component model of suppressible immune activity and more persistent endothelial injury. These findings qualify programs for prospective validation; they do not establish cell origin, efficacy, or clinical utility.
