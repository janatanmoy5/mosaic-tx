# Evidence landscape and novelty discipline

The project does not claim novelty from combining many tools. Its possible contribution is a focused
cross-cohort test of whether ABMR immune activity and tissue injury show separable behavior across DSA
states, rejection specificity and randomized treatment timepoints.

Before submission, perform a registered literature search covering DSA-positive accommodation,
ABMR molecular classifiers, FCGR3A/NK biology, endothelial injury, anti-CD38 treatment response and
patient-level single-cell transplant studies. The contribution statement must be rewritten against
the nearest published analyses. Reanalysis that only reproduces known findings is valuable validation
but not a novel mechanism.
