# Alignment with R01AI180118 without duplicating its aims

## Funded program

R01AI180118 develops transcriptomic drug-repositioning for ABMR, integrates public and newly
generated single-cell data, and advances single or combination candidates into human-cell/ex-vivo
and rodent ABMR validation.

## MOSAIC-Tx contribution

MOSAIC-Tx is positioned upstream of candidate discovery as an independent qualification layer:

```text
Published cohorts
  → reference-control sensitivity
  → cross-cohort replication
  → ABMR-versus-TCMR specificity
  → cell-state localization
  → treatment-trajectory consistency
  → qualified signature for R01 drug-reversal analysis
```

It does not claim ownership of the R01 drug-repositioning concept, newly generated UCSF single-cell
data, ex-vivo studies, animal validation or unpublished candidate compounds.

## Pilot value to the transplantation program

The executed pilot shows why signature qualification is necessary. All major immune/endothelial
modules replicated in ABMR versus non-rejection, but none was positively enriched in ABMR versus
TCMR. A naive drug-reversal pipeline could therefore prioritize drugs against general rejection or
inflammation rather than ABMR-specific biology.

The framework offers the program:

- a versioned public-dataset registry and checksum-verified compilation;
- frozen module definitions and explicit difficult controls;
- independent direction-aware replication;
- separation of DSA exposure, rejection, and treatment contrasts;
- patient-blocked analysis of the randomized felzartamab dataset;
- auditable null and contradictory results;
- a planned cell-state/reference-sensitivity gate before candidate discovery;
- reproducible tables and figures that can be regenerated on another system.

## Concrete collaboration request

The appropriate request is to evaluate whether this independent robustness framework could support
external validation or stress-testing of molecular signatures within R01AI180118. Any evaluation of
unpublished signatures or compounds would use a preregistered, preferably blinded protocol agreed
with the UCSF team.

## Data that would materially strengthen the work

- matched DSA specificity, strength, subclass and complement-binding information;
- Banff glomerulitis, peritubular capillaritis, C4d and chronic injury scores;
- CMV and BK status;
- donor/recipient HLA and recipient KIR information where available;
- cell-level counts plus patient and biopsy identifiers;
- independent ex-vivo or perturbational response data;
- an untouched external cohort for final signature assessment.

## Authorship and research-boundary principle

Public-data work performed before collaboration remains fully documented. New analyses using UCSF
data, unpublished grant outputs or candidate compounds require prior agreement on scope, governance,
authorship, data security and publication review. No clinical recommendation will be made from this
pilot.
