# Manuscript figure legends

## Figure 1. Prespecified study design
Cohort roles, biological estimands and stop/go logic. This panel contains no results.

## Figure 2. Qualification of a treatment-responsive rejection program
(A) GSE50084 effect estimates separate DSA exposure from rejection among DSA-positive grafts. (B) Cross-cohort concordance is displayed against ABMR-versus-TCMR specificity. (C) Hallmark GSEA direction across discovery, replication, specificity and randomized treatment contrasts. (D) Patient-blocked felzartamab-minus-placebo treatment-by-time interactions for published ABMR, IFN-gamma, NK and endothelial scores. Bars are approximate 95% confidence intervals; multiplicity is controlled by BH FDR within declared families.

## Figure 3. Sample-level verification
Unsupervised PCA, individual module-score distributions and longitudinal patient trajectories. These panels verify sample structure and labeling; they are descriptive and are not substitutes for Figure 2 inferential estimates.
